About Me

A Creative Visionary
Driven by Story

I'm Vanathi Krishnamoorthi — a multidisciplinary creative professional passionate about transforming ideas into cinematic visual experiences. My work spans graphic design, video editing, motion graphics, branding, and digital marketing.

I believe that great design isn't just about aesthetics — it's about storytelling. Every brand has a unique story, and I use design thinking, motion craft, and visual strategy to communicate that story with precision and power.

From building bold brand identities for local businesses to producing high-impact content for international clients in Switzerland, Poland, and Germany, I bring a global perspective with a deeply personal creative touch.

Currently leading creative teams at CloudFlake InfoTech Pvt Ltd, I bridge the gap between creativity and strategy — delivering experiences that inspire, engage, and convert.
